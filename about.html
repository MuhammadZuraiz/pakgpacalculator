About GPA Calculator Pakistan

Learn about GPA Calculator Pakistan, an independent student tool hub for GPA, CGPA, credit hours, and percentage guidance.

Why this site exists

GPA Calculator Pakistan is built to help students quickly estimate semester GPA, updated CGPA, credit-hour impact, and GPA-related questions.

The site focuses on practical calculator tools and plain-English explanations for Pakistan university students.

Accurate, university-specific scales

Each university page is set to the grading scale that university actually uses, including whether it relies on minus grades or relative grading, so the estimate is as close as a calculator can get.

We research grading scales from official regulations and reputable references, and we keep clear warnings that the official transcript is final.

Independent and unofficial

This website is not affiliated with NUST, FAST, COMSATS, IIUI, GIKI, PIEAS, Air University, UET, IST, or any other university.

Every calculator result should be treated as an estimate. Official transcripts, result portals, and academic offices are the final source.
