How grading works at IIUI

International Islamic University Islamabad uses absolute grading with the fixed marks bands above, on a half-point scale with no minus grades. The minimum passing grade is D (50%).

A BS degree requires a minimum CGPA of 2.00. A CGPA around 3.52 or above qualifies for the IIUI Dean's Honor List and strengthens merit-scholarship eligibility.

Worked example

Suppose this semester you take two 3-credit-hour courses and score an A (4.0) in one and a B+ (3.5) in the other. The quality points are (4.0 × 3) + (3.5 × 3) = 12 + 10.5 = 22.5.

Your semester GPA is 22.5 ÷ 6 = 3.75. If your previous CGPA was 3.20 over 60 completed credit hours, your updated CGPA is ((3.20 × 60) + 22.5) ÷ 66 = 3.25.

Get an accurate estimate

Pick the grade scale that matches your result card, enter every current-semester course with its exact credit hours, and keep lab and theory courses on separate rows when they carry separate credits. If your department uses a different grade-point table, treat the result as a planning estimate.

FAQ

IIUI GPA Calculator FAQ

Is this the official IIUI calculator?

No. It is an independent student tool and is not affiliated with International Islamic University Islamabad.

Does IIUI use minus grades?

No. IIUI uses a half-point scale (A, B+, B, C+, C, D+, D, F) with no minus grades, so keep the Pakistan no-minus scale selected.

What is the passing grade at IIUI?

D (1.0 grade points), which requires at least 50% marks. Below 50% is an F and the course must be retaken.

Is IIUI grading absolute or relative?

IIUI uses absolute grading, so your grade depends on your own marks rather than the class average.

What CGPA do I need for the Dean's Honor List?

Around 3.52 or above. Always confirm the current threshold with your faculty office.
