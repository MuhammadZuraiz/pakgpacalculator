How grading works at IIUI

International Islamic University Islamabad publishes the absolute grade table above on a half-point scale with no minus grades. The table assigns D (1.00) to 50–54 marks, but programme-specific course-completion and progression requirements can be stricter, so the calculator does not label a result as an official pass or fail.

The undergraduate regulations list 2.00 as the minimum CGPA for award of an undergraduate degree. Programme rules and later amendments remain authoritative.

Worked example

On the IIUI preset, grade A (4.00) in a 3-credit course and grade C (2.00) in a 1-credit course produce 12.00 + 2.00 = 14.00 quality points.

Divide 14.00 by 4 credits for a semester GPA of 3.50. With a previous CGPA of 3.20 over 60 credits, the simplified updated estimate is 3.22 before any university-specific repeat or exclusion rule.

Get an accurate estimate

Pick the grade scale that matches your result card, enter every current-semester course with its exact credit hours, and keep lab and theory courses on separate rows when they carry separate credits. If your department uses a different grade-point table, treat the result as a planning estimate.

FAQ

IIUI GPA Calculator FAQ

Is this the official IIUI calculator?

No. It is an independent student tool and is not affiliated with International Islamic University Islamabad.

Does IIUI use minus grades?

No. The cited IIUI table uses a half-point scale with no minus grades. This page starts with the IIUI-specific preset selected.

What does the IIUI table assign at 50%?

The cited undergraduate table maps 50–54 marks to D (1.00 point). Check your programme's current course-completion requirement before treating that letter as sufficient.

Is IIUI grading absolute or relative?

IIUI uses absolute grading, so your grade depends on your own marks rather than the class average.

How does IIUI calculate GPA?

The regulations use the sum of each course's credit hours multiplied by its grade point, divided by the total attempted credit hours.
