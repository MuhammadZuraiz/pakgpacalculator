Editorial standards

Methodology & Sources

See how GPA Calculator Pakistan calculates results, selects university sources, tests grade scales, and handles corrections and limitations.

Calculation method

For every entered course, quality points equal grade points multiplied by credit hours. Semester GPA equals total quality points divided by total attempted semester credits.

Updated CGPA is estimated as ((previous CGPA × the earlier GPA-bearing credits used for that CGPA) + current quality points) ÷ (earlier GPA credits + current GPA credits). Calculations use full precision internally and display two decimals.

Source hierarchy

Current official university regulations, handbooks, and registrar notifications come first. An official university academic page is used when a current document is not public. Secondary calculator sites are not accepted as evidence for a grade scale.

If a source does not publish fixed percentage bands, this site does not invent them. A blank marks column means students should use the letter grade on the official result.

Review ownership

The GPA Calculator Pakistan editorial maintainer owns the source checks and calculator corrections recorded here. Questions and evidence-backed corrections can be sent to pakgpacalculator@gmail.com.

What the estimate cannot know

A simple calculator cannot automatically reconstruct repeat-course replacement, excluded W/I grades, transfer credits, cohort exceptions, relative-grading boundaries, or a department's rounding procedure.

That is why each university page identifies its source scope and sends students back to the examination office for a final decision.

Verification checklist

  • Each source-specific preset contains exactly the grades and point values shown in its visible table.
  • Worked examples are checked with the same weighted-average formula used by the calculator.
  • Zero-point F grades remain valid courses when credits are entered.
  • Negative credits, out-of-range CGPA, and incomplete previous-record pairs are rejected.
  • Official source links and calculator behavior are rechecked when a correction is received or a newer regulation is found.
