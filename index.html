How to use this GPA calculator

Enter every current-semester course with its grade and credit hours. The calculator multiplies each grade point by the course credit hours, adds the course points, and divides them by the current semester credits.

  1. Select the grading scale used by your university or department.
  2. Add each subject, grade, and credit hours from the current semester.
  3. Enter previous CGPA and completed credit hours if you want updated CGPA.
  4. Use print when you need a simple copy of the result.

GPA vs CGPA

GPA usually means the average for one semester or term. CGPA means cumulative GPA across all completed semesters. That is why CGPA needs the credit hours you completed before the current semester.

Course points: grade points x credit hours

GPA: total course points / semester credit hours

CGPA: total cumulative points / total completed credits

Example calculation

If you take two 3-credit courses and score A in one and B+ in the other, your semester points are 12 + 10.5 = 22.5. Divide 22.5 by 6 credits and the semester GPA is 3.75.

If your previous CGPA was 3.20 over 60 credits, the updated CGPA is ((3.20 x 60) + 22.5) / 66 = 3.25.

Questions students ask

GPA calculator FAQ

What should I enter as completed credit hours?

Enter the credit hours you completed before this semester. For example, if you passed 60 credits before the current semester, enter 60. Do not include current-semester credits in that field.

Can I calculate more than 15 courses?

Yes. Start with the default rows and use Add course when needed. This version supports up to 30 course rows, which covers most semester and yearly course loads.

How do I convert GPA to percentage?

GPA to percentage conversion is university-dependent. Some universities publish their own formula, while others do not convert GPA directly. Use the rule printed by your university, department, or HEC-attested transcript guidance.

Why is my official GPA different from this calculator?

Differences usually come from grade scale, rounding, repeated courses, failed course policies, lab/theory credit splits, or department-specific transcript rules. Match the scale and credit hours carefully before comparing the result.

Does this calculator save my data?

No. The calculation happens in your browser. This site does not require accounts and does not store your course list or result.
