Privacy policy

Privacy Policy

GPA Calculator Pakistan is built as a simple browser-based calculator. You can calculate GPA and CGPA without creating an account or sending your course details to a server.

Information we collect

The calculator runs locally in your browser. Course names, grades, credit hours, previous CGPA, and the GPA-bearing credits counted behind it are used only to show your result on the page. This site does not require login details and does not store your calculator entries in a database.

Analytics and hosting logs

This site is hosted by Netlify. Like other web hosts, Netlify may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser or device details, requested URL, referring page, timestamp, and security or error data to deliver and protect the site. No separate audience-analytics service is configured on this site as of 12 August 2026.

Browser storage

If you switch between light and dark themes, the preference is stored in your browser under gpa-theme. It does not contain your name, course entries, grades, or calculated result. You can clear it through your browser's site-data controls.

Google advertising cookies

This site includes Google AdSense code on substantive calculator and guide pages. If Google serves advertising, Google and participating third-party vendors may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses, or similar technologies to select, deliver, measure, and personalize ads, subject to applicable consent choices and Google's policies. Google may use visits to this and other sites when personalizing advertising.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's ad settings. Users may also visit aboutads.info to learn about choices for some participating third-party vendors. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

External links

This site may link to external resources, such as official university pages or advertising preference pages. Those websites have their own privacy practices and are not controlled by GPA Calculator Pakistan.

Children and students

The calculator is intended as a general educational utility. Students should avoid entering private personal identifiers in subject name fields. A simple course title such as Calculus or Programming is enough.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when new features, analytics, or advertising services are added. The latest version will be available on this page. Last updated: 12 August 2026.
