Privacy policy

Privacy Policy

GPA Calculator Pakistan is built as a simple browser-based calculator. You can calculate GPA and CGPA without creating an account or sending your course details to a server.

Information we collect

The calculator runs locally in your browser. Course names, grades, credit hours, previous CGPA, and completed credits are used only to show your result on the page. This site does not require login details and does not store your calculator entries in a database.

Analytics and hosting logs

If analytics or standard hosting logs are enabled in the future, they may collect basic technical information such as page views, device type, browser, referring page, approximate region, and error logs. This helps maintain the site and improve the calculator experience.

Google advertising cookies

If Google AdSense or another Google advertising product is added to this website, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Users may also visit aboutads.info to learn about choices for some third-party vendors' personalized ads.

External links

This site may link to external resources, such as official university pages or advertising preference pages. Those websites have their own privacy practices and are not controlled by GPA Calculator Pakistan.

Children and students

The calculator is intended as a general educational utility. Students should avoid entering private personal identifiers in subject name fields. A simple course title such as Calculus or Programming is enough.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when new features, analytics, or advertising services are added. The latest version will be available on this page.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the official contact channel listed where this website is published.
